01 Logline & Project in Brief

THE LAST KNOT is a dark open-world action-RPG in anime pseudo-realism. The Woven Lands hold exactly seven Teocartridges — relics of godly memory and power that grant their holder longevity and authority over a region. Looming over all seven is Silk, a very physical plague: it corrupts electronics, rots the long-lived from within, and turns gods into puppets. The hero is a warm-blooded mortal with almost no implants — a "clean carrier": he does not collect relics, he cuts out infected knots to stop the plague, honor his murdered teacher's legacy, and bring justice to those who destroyed his home.

04 Why Now

  • The souls vibe is mainstream. Souls-likes grew from a niche cult to the mainstream; Sensor Tower tracks them as a separate growing category.
  • The gacha model is validated. Open world + gacha has proven multi-year retention (hundreds of millions of MAU at the leaders, tens of billions in category revenue).
  • The "adult" gacha niche is empty. Cuteness dominates; the 18–35 audience that grew up on souls-likes and hip-hop has no dark gacha open-world game in their language.
  • Cross-platform on Unity. One art line for PC/console/mobile, a fast iterative pipeline — lower risk for the vertical slice.
  • Clean regional builds. Global M-rated version plus a softened CN build — licensing cleanliness and premium positioning from day one.

06 Core Gameplay

Main loop (open world)

  • Open-world exploration: regions, verticality, "threads of comfort" rest points as souls-style checkpoints.
  • "Torn threads" — local rifts: a weave-puzzle or a combat "poisoned root".
  • Glitch hotspots — infection zones: implant skills work at risk, teleports are blocked; clean points discharge Silk.
  • Boss duel ("duel theater") — the peak of a session; co-op on regional gods.

Combat: souls vibe inside open-world mechanics

  • Thread elements — 4 types (Sun, Blood, Ice, Shadow) with paired reactions.
  • Parrying — a timed block; a perfect parry freezes the enemy ("weave tear").
  • Duel theater — bosses "invitingly open up": feigned availability is punished by parrying.
  • Balance principle: "an overleveled hero wins regardless of skill", but skill wins more beautifully and with margin — no resource loss on death.

07 Hero & Tone

Warm, not cold

Jokes, needling, helps first and asks later. "I'm a scoundrel, but I'll pull you out" — his motto.

Clean carrier

Almost no implants — nothing for Silk to latch onto; a "god thief" in looks, a purger in truth.

Goal: righteous justice

Not pure revenge: stop the plague, honor the teacher's legacy, restore the regions. Three layers: righteous / world / detective.

Venomous beauty

Costumes are a language of threat, not seduction: openness = a trap. No sex, within M rating.

Fan service without breaking style

Scenes, poses, the "clan room", and loaded dialogue — but not a gram of stat benefit: fan service never touches balance, pace, or the duel vibe (section 10).

  • Collection: "Teacher's Echoes" (12), "thread knots" (resources), Puppeteer lore fragments.

10 Monetization

LayerWhat we sell
PlaystyleNew "combat kernels" (stance/parry/combo as tangible mechanics) — the main gacha hook.
Contracts & invitationsRegional 5★ join with their story; play as the character or send on auto-dispatch missions.

Venom wearOpen "venomous" outfits within an M rating, no sex, no Adults Only.
Monetization boundaries: no pay-to-win in duels (numbers grow by farming, skill decides the beauty); "overleveling" as a whale motive without breaking balance; quests and story lines are fully available to everyone — the gacha drives the roster, not a story paywall.

Fan service: style stays intact

  • The "clan room" — a restored school/region: mix heroes, watch scenes and reactions. Rewards are cosmetics and flavor, never combat stats.
  • Duel poses and scene-flex — victory stances, capes, a photo mode "for screenshots"; part of the playstyle gacha (combat kernels), not a separate genre.
  • Reactions and choices — relationship fan service (loaded dialogue, epilogues), within M rating and "venomous beauty" as a language of threat, not seduction.
  • Genre easter eggs — collaborations and references only in cosmetics; never in the story or the balance.
  • Hard rule: fan service gives no combat numbers, does not block content, and requires no farming — it is flavor, not mechanics.

13 Budget & The Ask

The realistic bar for the first conversations is $500,000 (not millions): no one backs a "blank concept" with a big check. $500k turns the project into a playable demo that opens the door to Series A.

Engineering & infrastructure~10%Unity prototypes, server stub, demo deployment

What $500k buys: a vertical slice of Region 1 — a playable demo (one complete zone, duel boss, art line, core economy and gacha-contract systems) plus a pitch package. This is the base for Series A: a publisher check of $2–5M for CBT/beta and a 4+ region release (30–36 months). The full cycle is not "millions now" but two milestone checks: a small one for proof, a big one for scale.

The Ask

  • A first check of $500k for the vertical slice, paid in milestones per §11 (pre-production → demo).
  • An option on Series A ($2–5M) at the demo milestone: first-priority right to enter scaling.
  • Co-publishing & platform expertise: launch marketing, localization (EN/RU/JP/KR/DE), storefronts, dual censorship builds (Global M / softened CN).
  • QA/live-ops and community: beta support, balance loops, analytics tooling.

What the publisher gets

  • The only discerning "adult" open-world gacha title in the portfolio — an 18+ audience with no overlap with casual brands.
  • A proven design foundation: the full GDD v1.0 + pitch package + a vertical-slice plan; a fast start with no "empty concept".
  • A long content tail: seasons and an IP line (merch, soundtrack, collaborations) inside the ROI structure.
